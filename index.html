Privacy Policy

MultiCam OneShot

Last updated: September 10, 2026

MultiCam OneShot respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

Camera and Microphone

MultiCam OneShot may request access to your device's cameras and microphone solely to provide photo and video capture features. Camera and microphone data are processed locally on your device.

Photos

The app may request permission to save photos and videos to your photo library. Photos and videos created using MultiCam OneShot remain on your device according to the permissions you grant.

Data Collection

MultiCam OneShot does not require an account and does not collect personal information, analytics data, location data, or advertising identifiers.

Tracking and Advertising

MultiCam OneShot does not track users and does not contain third-party advertising or advertising trackers.

Permissions

You can manage or revoke camera, microphone, and photo permissions at any time through the iOS Settings app.

Children's Privacy

MultiCam OneShot does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.
