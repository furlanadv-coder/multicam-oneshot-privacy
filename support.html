Camera Permission

MultiCam OneShot requires access to the device cameras to provide its multi-camera photo and video capture functionality.

You can review camera permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

Microphone Permission

Microphone access is used when recording videos with audio.

You can review microphone permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Photos Permission

Photos permission allows MultiCam OneShot to save the photos and videos you capture to your photo library.

You can review photo permissions in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Troubleshooting

If MultiCam OneShot is not working as expected:

  • Verify that Camera, Microphone and Photos permissions are enabled.
  • Close and reopen MultiCam OneShot.
  • Restart your device if the issue continues.
  • Make sure your device is running a current version of iOS.
